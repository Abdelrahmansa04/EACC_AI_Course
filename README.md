# EACC AI Course

A beginner-friendly Artificial Intelligence course created for **AI Explorers Academy** at EACC.
The course introduces young learners to AI through simple explanations, real-life examples, interactive activities, and classroom discussions.

## About the Course

This repository contains the first phase of the AI course for kids.
The goal is to help students understand AI in a simple, visual, and interactive way.

Students learn that AI is not magic.
AI is a tool made by people.
AI learns from examples, finds repeated things, and makes smart guesses.

## Target Age Group

This material is designed for young learners, mainly ages **4–9**.

The lessons use:

* Simple language
* Visual examples
* Interactive games
* Classroom discussion
* Short activities
* Real-life examples kids already know

## Phase 1 Overview

Phase 1 introduces students to the basic ideas of Artificial Intelligence.

### Session 1: Introduction to AI

**Main idea:**
AI is a smart computer program made by people.

Students learn:

* What AI means
* Where we see AI in daily life
* The difference between humans and AI
* Examples of AI tools around us

Activity:

* **AI Hunt Game**
* Students identify which objects use AI and which do not.

Examples:

* Face ID
* YouTube
* Google Maps
* Smart TV
* Voice assistants
* Games

---

### Session 2: How AI Learns

**Main idea:**
AI learns from examples.

Students learn:

* AI needs examples to learn
* AI gets better with practice
* AI is like a football player who trains many times
* Good examples help AI learn better

Activity:

* Interactive computer activity about how AI learns
* Students explore examples and choices to understand learning through practice.

Simple classroom message:

> AI learns by seeing many examples.

---

### Session 3: AI Finds Patterns

**Main idea:**
AI looks carefully, finds repeated things, and guesses what comes next.

Students learn:

* AI looks at examples
* AI sees the same thing again and again
* AI can guess what comes next
* Simple patterns using colors, animals, and real-life examples

Activity:

* Interactive “what comes next?” computer game
* Students help a robot choose the next picture
* Includes colors, animals, YouTube example, phone unlock example, and a final challenge

Simple classroom message:

> Look. Think. Choose.

## Session Timing

Each session is designed to be short and practical.

Typical timing:

```text
10 minutes   Warm-up, intro, and discussion
12–15 minutes Interactive activity
2–3 minutes  Quick recap
```

Total session time:

```text
22–25 minutes
```

## Course Goals

By the end of Phase 1, students should understand:

* AI is made by people
* AI is not magic
* AI learns from examples
* AI can find repeated things
* AI can make smart guesses
* AI is used in many tools around us

## Teaching Style

The course is designed to be:

* Simple
* Visual
* Fun
* Interactive
* Discussion-based
* Suitable for young learners

The teacher should use short questions, real examples, and hands-on activities.

## Example Teaching Language

Use simple sentences such as:

```text
AI looks carefully.
AI learns from examples.
AI sees the same thing again and again.
AI guesses what comes next.
```

Avoid difficult words at the beginning.
For young students, say:

```text
same thing again and again
```

Then later introduce:

```text
pattern
```
